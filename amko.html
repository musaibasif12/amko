FAQs

Clear answers, confident decisions

Get clear answers to common questions about pricing, timelines, materials, and supervision.

What construction services does AMKO provide?

Grey structure construction, complete/turnkey construction with material, design & planning, and home renovation.

Do you handle complete house construction?

Yes — from grey structure to fully finished, turnkey construction with material, all the way to handover.

How much does house construction cost per square foot?

Grey structure starts around Rs 2,500/sq. ft., and complete/turnkey construction around Rs 4,500/sq. ft. Final pricing depends on design and material specifications — use the estimator above for a range on your plot.

Which areas does AMKO serve?

DHA, Naya Nazimabad, Bahria Town, Scheme 33, Saadi Town, Saadi Garden and nearby areas across Karachi.

How long does it take to complete a house?

Timelines depend on plot size and scope of work. We agree a realistic schedule with you in writing before construction starts.

Can I use my own architectural design or map?

Yes, you're welcome to bring your own approved map or design. If you don't have one yet, our team can handle design and planning for you.

How do you ensure construction quality?

Every project is personally supervised by our engineering team, with defined material specifications and scope agreed before work begins.

Can I visit your completed projects?

Yes — we're happy to arrange a visit to a completed or ongoing project so you can see the work firsthand before deciding.
